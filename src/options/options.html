Alerts

You get one alert per page load while a site is affected. To silence a site, pick a duration from the alert, the popup or the banner (1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, or for good). Those choices are listed under Ignored sites below.

Not seeing alerts? Send a test one. On Windows also check Focus Assist / Do Not Disturb and Settings → System → Notifications for your browser.

Privacy & scope

  • PHPLens reads the Server, X-Powered-By, Set-Cookie, X-Generator, X-Pingback, Link and cache headers of responses your browser already received, plus page hints (URLs ending in .php, generator meta tag, CMS resource paths, visible PHP error output, phpinfo pages).
  • Apart from the single HEAD fallback above (which you can switch off), it never sends requests to the sites you visit, never submits data and never attempts to trigger the vulnerability.
  • Per-tab results are kept in session storage and are gone when the browser closes. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • A hidden version (expose_php=Off) is reported as Unverified, not as safe.
